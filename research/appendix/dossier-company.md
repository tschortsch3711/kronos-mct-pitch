# company

## Summary
KRONOS Mechatronics GmbH (brand "Kronos-MCT", tagline "Printing the future in every dimension") is a Nürnberg machine builder for 3D printed electronics / Additive Manufactured Electronics (AME), founded in 2024 by CEO Jörg Dickerboom and registered at Amtsgericht Nürnberg, HRB 43784 (sole representation by Dickerboom since 22 Nov 2024; two shareholders; share capital raised to EUR 50,000 on 19 Dec 2024). The pivotal fact for any pitch: KRONOS is effectively the phoenix of Neotech AMT GmbH, the Nürnberg pioneer of 5-axis 3D printed electronics (technology development since 2009, world's first 5-axis 3D-PE system installed 2010, patented 45X mass-production platform, first commercial mass-production install of printed 3D antennas capable of millions of parts/year). Neotech AMT entered insolvency in July/September 2024 (Amtsgericht Nürnberg IN 771/24, administrator Dr. Harald Schwartz), and KRONOS acquired its assets in 2024, then updated and relaunched the Helios (compact entry/prototyping) and Hyperion (industrial, small-series production) 5-axis AME systems under the KRONOS name. The team includes ex-research talent: Daniel Ahlers (Uni Hamburg PhD researcher on nonplanar slicing, 5-axis printer calibration, and CNN-based in-situ verification of printed electronics — now Software Development Engineer at KRONOS) and Stefan Werner (FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg). Technology: modular 5-axis platforms (±5 µm accuracy, <1 m/s motion, linear motors/encoders) combining piezo jetting, inkjet, aerosol deposition, dispensing, FFF, wire encapsulation, plasma cleaning, UV curing, CNC milling and SMD pick-and-place; materials from nanoparticle silver inks to high-viscosity pastes; proprietary Aion-5X CAD/CAM (5-axis simultaneous + 3+2 indexed toolpaths, collision-detection simulation, ISO G-code post-processor) plus AI-supported image processing. Traction: incubated at nkubator (N-ERGIE/City of Nürnberg green-tech incubator "Auf AEG", joined ~March 2025); strategic North America partnership with APES announced 8 Apr 2025 at RAPID+TCT (APES resells Helios/Hyperion and adopts Aion-5X, including for its Matrix6D platform); listed distributor NTV USA LLC; exhibited at productronica 2025 (Nov 18–21); won the LOPEC 2026 Start-up Award "Best Business Potential" (25 Feb 2026), positioned in in-mold electronics for automotive. Revenue model (per OE-A/LOPEC): machine sales + Aion-5X licensing + application development, process integration and maintenance; long-term recurring software, OEM integrations, industry solution packages; services span PoC studies to EMS. No VC funding found; company holds an EU Funding & Tenders Portal PIC. Pricing: no public KRONOS prices; closest anchors are Neotech 15X BT "from $215,000 (€200,000)" (Aniwaa), Nano Dimension DragonFly IV ~$400k, Optomec ~$333k/unit implied by a $2M six-printer order. Nürnberg ecosystem ties are deep: Neotech legacy includes the TÜV SÜD Innovation Award 2019 jointly with FAU's FAPS institute and the BMBF project A4I-PE3D (with Conti Temic, GSB-Wahl, Holst Centre/TNO); FAPS still operates a Neotech 15XSA system.
