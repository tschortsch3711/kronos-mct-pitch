TOTALS: (a) Professional AME machine market = ~45-70 units/yr worldwide, ~EUR 12-22M/yr hardware revenue. Adding desktop additive (Voltera NOVA + misc) gives ~100-190 total AME units/yr and ~EUR 15-28M/yr. This confirms the prior: tens of professional units, low tens of millions EUR. (b) EUR 25-100k band: demonstrated demand is essentially one product — Voltera NOVA (~US$46k, launched 2023), whose unit count is NOT public; my estimate is 40-90 units/yr (100-250 cumulative by end-2025), low confidence, and this is the single most important number to verify via primary outreach. Band total demonstrated demand: ~50-120 units/yr = EUR 2.5-6M/yr, overwhelmingly academic. VERDICT: The observed market does NOT support a venture-scale outcome on machine sales. A realistic winner in the EUR 25-100k band reaches EUR 1-5M/yr within 3-4 years and tops out at an LPKF-style EUR 20-30M/yr niche only if it converts part of the subtractive-prototyping installed base and adds a consumables annuity. Two structural warnings dominate: the market leader (Nano Dimension) exited additive electronics via asset sale, and the direct 5-axis predecessor in KRONOS's own city (Neotech) went insolvent in 2024. Any venture thesis must rest on market creation (production applications: antennas, sensors, defense), not on the ~100-190 units/yr that exist today.

Privately held; third-party estimates (Owler/D&B-type) put revenue around US$20-40M with ~150 employees, but this includes non-AME laser-cladding business added via the Huffman (2018) and CDS (2020) acquisitions, so AME machine revenue is likely US$10-20M.

### Optomec customer split [low]

### Bottom-up total (b): EUR 25-100k band demand [medium]
Demonstrated annual unit demand in the EUR 25-100k additive band is ~50-120 units/yr worldwide (~EUR 2.5-6M/yr), of which Voltera NOVA is the overwhelming majority; the remainder is scattered one-off academic purchases (BotFactory-class tools sit below the band at <US$20k). A new entrant like KRONOS realistically captures 10-30 units/yr (EUR 0.5-2.5M revenue) within 3-4 years absent market creation.
SOURCE: Bottom-up synthesis (this analysis) — https://www.voltera.io/nova

### Upside ceiling via subtractive conversion [medium]
The only large adjacent machine population at this price point is subtractive PCB prototyping (LPKF ProtoMat plus Bantam Tools-class mills): several hundred units/yr, EUR 10-20M/yr of spend. Even full conversion of this demand plus consumables annuity caps a band winner at an LPKF-Development-style EUR 20-30M/yr business — a solid Mittelstand niche, not a venture-scale (EUR 100M+) outcome.
SOURCE: Synthesis: LPKF Development segment as ceiling analog (this analysis) — https://www.lpkf.com/en/investor-relations/reports-presentations

### Structural red flags for venture thesis [medium]
The two most-capitalized attempts at this exact market both failed to make it venture-scale: Nano Dimension (raised >US$1.5bn) abandoned/divested DragonFly after ~60-80 lifetime units, and Neotech AMT — the direct 5-axis predecessor headquartered in KRONOS's own city of Nürnberg — went insolvent in 2024 after ~50 units in 20 years. Optomec, the category leader, remains a ~US$20-40M private company after 25+ years.
SOURCE: Synthesis of Nano Dimension filings, Neotech insolvency coverage, Optomec profile (this analysis) — https://investors.nano-di.com/

### Academic vs industrial demand structure [medium]
Across every vendor with visible customer lists (Optomec AJ, XTPL, Neotech, Ceradrop, Voltera NOVA), the estimated academic/research-institute share of AME machine placements is 50-80%. Purchases are grant-funded, one-per-lab, and non-repeating — explaining why no vendor has ever exceeded ~20 units/yr in the professional segment and why consumables/ink pull-through has stayed small.
SOURCE: Cross-vendor synthesis (this analysis) — https://optomec.com/printed-electronics/aerosol-jet-technology/
